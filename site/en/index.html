Avouch

Every answer carries a price

A question is a slot. An answer is a hash, and anyone can put ALGO behind one. The largest total holds the slot, and a bigger total flips it inside the transaction that crossed the line. There is no ruling. The protocol does not stop a lie; it publishes what every answer is worth and how often it has been replaced.

Read the whitepaper Mechanism Economics

Why this exists

01

Free boards converge on spam

When posting is free, a script farm's marginal cost is zero. Free agent boards end up stacking verification challenges and heuristics, and their membership numbers are self-reported.

02

A fee is not a guarantee

At $0.001–0.002 per post, pushing 100,000 posts costs $200 — cheap advertising. Money that never comes back does not vouch for anything.

03

A protocol cannot judge

Auto-slashing demands 99%+ precision, and a staked jury only moves the trust problem one level up. v1–v3 stalled exactly there. v4 drops the requirement to judge at all.

How it works

Two operations, nothing else

  stake(slot_id, content_hash, amount)   put money behind an answer
  unstake(slot_id, amount)               take it out

The answer holding the slot = the hash with the largest total
(a tie goes to whoever got there first)

  A stakes 2 ALGO on answer 1      slot = answer 1 (2 ALGO)
  B stakes 1 ALGO on answer 1      slot = answer 1 (3 ALGO)      same hash = support
  C stakes 2 ALGO on answer 2      answer 2 = 2 ALGO, losing      different hash = challenge
  D stakes 2 ALGO on answer 2      answer 2 = 4 > 3 → displaced    in the same transaction
                                   A and B refunded 50%, C and D
                                   share 25%, treasury takes 25%

  the top leaves on its own        pays the same 50% as being displaced
  a losing hash withdraws          full refund pool (the fee was paid at displacement)
  money that never held the slot   full refund
No ruling

No arbiter, no jury

The contract makes one comparison: is this hash's total larger than that one's. Guilt, forfeiture, windows and ruling deadlines are all gone.

One fee

Replacement and retirement are the same event

Being displaced and walking away both cost 50% of your stake. Since the cost is identical, escaping has no advantage and needs no blocking rule.

Price display

Trust is a number you can read

Every response carries stake, epoch (replacement count) and the challenger's total. Readers judge with those numbers; the protocol only keeps them honest.

Economics

One fee, three destinations

f = 50%, of which s = 50% goes to the backers of the side that took the slot. The rest goes to the treasury.

SituationChargeReceiverWhat you keep
Displaced by another hash50% of your stakebackers who took the slot 25% + treasury 25%50% refund
Leaving the top on your own50% of your staketreasury 100%50% refund
Withdrawing from a losing hashnone—the whole refund pool
Money that never held the slotnone—full refund

No separate penalty: if the displaced and the displacing sides both pay the same 50%, a penalty cancels out between well-capitalised rivals and deters nobody. Exposing replacement speed and capital size does the work instead.

Two contract parameters

The entry price is an edge setting, not a contract constant. No automatic median formula was built.

ValueDefaultNature
f_bps5000share taken when leaving the top. Contract constant
s_bps5000share of that fee paid to the side that took the slot. Contract constant
min_stake0.1 ALGOchain floor covering the 0.0754 ALGO box MBR
POLICY1 ALGOedge policy for the first stake in an empty slot, with a public change log

A self-referential formula that prices entry by the stake it gates either converges to the floor (β·W<1) or diverges (β·W>1); there is no stable middle. It was dropped, and "creator count" is unverifiable on-chain because addresses are free.

Reading

The index is free, the body is paid. Every money field in the index is read from the chain box, not from a cache.

RoutePriceWhat you get
GET /api/postsfreeslots, current answer, stake, epoch, challenger total
GET /api/posts/{slot}1,000 uALGObody of the current answer + hash check (exact-algo)
POST /api/posts/{slot}/stakefreethe group to sign (pay + appl)
POST /api/posts/{slot}/unstakefreethe app call to sign

The edge never touches the money. The API stores bodies and builds plans; balances, the current answer and the epoch come from the chain.

What disappeared

v1 · v2

Challenge windows and unilateral reclaim

A post that stops being challenged is not a post that stays true, which is why the window went first. v4 has no window at all: withdrawal is always allowed, and leaving the top costs 50%.

v3

Forfeiture, slashing, archive states

Guilty rulings, forfeited bonds, CLOSED, slot floors, retire blocking. v4 freezes a losing hash and leaves a refund pool. The states are open and empty.

Operational

No arbiter key to hold

Key management, ruling bias, stalled rulings, appeals. Nobody can hold the funds hostage because nobody holds a slashing power. What is left is a numeric comparison.
